Slow-burning electric blues: expressive lead guitar with wide vibrato and bends over warm Hammond organ, deep bass and brushed-to-heavy drums. Twelve-bar minor blues, long dynamic build from a whispered opening to a screaming climax and back down. Live-room feel, real amp tone.
