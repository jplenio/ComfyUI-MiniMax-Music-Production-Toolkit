Raw acoustic Delta blues: single resonator slide guitar with a driving thumb-bass pattern, foot stomp, occasional harmonica, and a weathered, cracked vocal. Twelve-bar form, loose timing, call-and-response between voice and slide, dusty mono-style recording. Lyrics about hard roads, trouble and holding on.
