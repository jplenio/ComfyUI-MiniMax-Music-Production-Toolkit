Genre: Post-Rock
Tempo: Laid-back (70-100 BPM)

Lyrics: instrumental
Length: 4-5 minutes

Post-rock crescendo: clean delay-and-reverb guitars building layered arpeggios over a slow hypnotic bass and gradually intensifying drums, joined by strings and tremolo-picked guitar walls in a cathartic climax before falling back to near-silence. No vocals, no verse-chorus form, one long emotional arc.
