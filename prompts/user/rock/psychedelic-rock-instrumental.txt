1960s psychedelic rock: fuzz guitar with wah and heavy phasing, swirling Hammond organ, sitar-like drones, backwards tape effects, loose live drums and a long hypnotic modal jam. Wide panning trickery, a slow build into a chaotic peak and a woozy fade. Warm analog tape sound.
