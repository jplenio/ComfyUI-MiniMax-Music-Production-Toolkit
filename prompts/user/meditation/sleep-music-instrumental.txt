Genre: Sleep Music
Tempo: Slow (40-70 BPM)
Meter: free time / rubato
Lyrics: instrumental

Sleep music: extremely soft low-frequency pads, distant muted piano, subtle sub-bass warmth and almost inaudible movement. No percussion, no melody that draws attention, very gradual fade-in and fade-out. Dark, warm and enveloping.
