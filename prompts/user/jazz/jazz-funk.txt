Genre: Jazz Fusion
Tempo: Midtempo (100-120 BPM)
Meter: 4/4 (common time)
Lyrics: instrumental
Length: 4-5 minutes

Sophisticated, extremely groovy jazz-funk: Fender Rhodes, funky sixteenth-note guitar, warm tenor saxophone lead, syncopated melodic bass and tight acoustic drums with ghost notes. Catchy recurring riffs, rich extended harmony, tasteful solos and playful interaction between the instruments. Live-band feel with polished 1970s production.
