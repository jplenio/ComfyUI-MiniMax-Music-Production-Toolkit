Hard 1970s P-Funk: syncopated slap bass locked to a heavy one-drop kick and snare, wah-wah guitar sixteenths, clavinet stabs, greasy Hammond organ and a punchy horn section trading riffs. One repeating groove with breakdowns, vamps and a screaming sax solo. Raw analog live-band sound.
