Extremely groovy and joyful nu-disco funk: infectious slap-and-mute bassline, tight sixteenth-note funk guitar, rhythmic Rhodes and clavinet, punchy drums with open hi-hats, catchy synth riffs and string stabs. Highly recognizable hooks, playful interplay between instruments, filtered breakdown and a full-band final chorus. Polished, wide and irresistible.
