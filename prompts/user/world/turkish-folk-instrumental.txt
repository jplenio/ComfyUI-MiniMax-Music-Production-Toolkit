---
Genre: Turkish Folk
Tempo: Midtempo (100-120 BPM)
Meter: 9/8
Lyrics: instrumental
Length: 3-4 minutes
---

Anatolian Turkish folk: bağlama / saz lead with fast tremolo and microtonal ornaments, ney flute, kemençe, darbuka and davul percussion in an asymmetric rhythm. Modal makam melody, a free-rhythm improvised opening, then a driving dance section.
