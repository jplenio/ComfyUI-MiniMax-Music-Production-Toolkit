---
Genre: Gnawa
Tempo: Midtempo (100-120 BPM)
Meter: 6/8
Lyrics: only voice - no words
Voice: group vocals / gang vocals
Length: 4-5 minutes
---

Moroccan Gnawa trance: deep booming guembri bass lute repeating a hypnotic riff, metallic qraqeb castanets driving a relentless shuffle, and call-and-response wordless group chanting. Very gradual acceleration, hypnotic repetition, ceremonial and entrancing.
