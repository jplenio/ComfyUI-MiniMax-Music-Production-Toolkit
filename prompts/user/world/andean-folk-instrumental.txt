---
Genre: Andean Folk
Tempo: Midtempo (100-120 BPM)
Meter: 2/4 (march / polka)
Lyrics: instrumental
Length: 3-4 minutes

Andean folk: breathy quena and zampoña panpipes carrying a pentatonic melody, charango tremolo, nylon guitar, bombo legüero drum and gentle shaker. Wide mountain-air atmosphere, melancholic yet dancing, a slow lament opening into a lively huayno rhythm.
