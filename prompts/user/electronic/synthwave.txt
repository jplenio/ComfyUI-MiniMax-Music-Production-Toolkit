Nostalgic, cinematic synthwave: warm analog synthesizers, pulsing octave bass, LinnDrum-style vintage drum machine, shimmering arpeggios and a powerful memorable lead melody. Strong recurring motifs, gradual layering and a dreamy neon nighttime atmosphere. Authentic 1980s character with modern wide production.
