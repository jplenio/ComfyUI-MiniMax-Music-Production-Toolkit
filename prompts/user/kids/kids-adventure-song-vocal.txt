Energetic kids adventure song: driving acoustic and light electric guitar, marching drums, brass stabs, whistling hook and an enthusiastic lead vocal with a shouting children's chorus. Playful, exciting and easy to sing along, with a rhythmic chant section. Lyrics about setting off on a journey, being brave and sticking together.
