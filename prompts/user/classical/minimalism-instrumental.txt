Minimalist piece in the Glass and Reich tradition: fast repeating arpeggiated cells on piano and marimba, slowly shifting phase and additive rhythm, layered woodwinds and strings entering one by one. Hypnotic pulse, very gradual harmonic change, bright and mesmerizing.
