Norteño corrido: lead diatonic accordion and bajo sexto, tuba or electric bass and a marching polka-style drum beat. Storytelling verses sung by two voices in close parallel harmony, with accordion answers between lines. Proud, narrative and rustic, live-band recording. Lyrics telling the story of one person and a decisive event in their life.
