Romantic bachata: nylon and requinto guitar arpeggios with the characteristic lead fills, bongó and güira percussion, warm bass and a heartfelt slightly nasal vocal. Four-beat pattern with the hip accent on beat four, intimate verses and a soaring chorus. Lyrics about longing, devotion and a love that hurts and heals.
