---
Genre: Polka
Tempo: Uptempo (130-145 BPM)
Meter: 2/4 (march / polka)
Lyrics: instrumental
Length: 2-3 minutes
---

Fast traditional polka: accordion and clarinet melody, oompah tuba and snare backbeat, trumpet harmonies and a bouncing two-beat groove. Bright major key, clear A-B-C sections with repeats, joyful and irresistibly danceable.
