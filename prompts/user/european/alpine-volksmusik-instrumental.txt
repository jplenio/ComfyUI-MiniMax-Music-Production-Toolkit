Traditional Alpine Volksmusik: diatonic button accordion and Steirische harmonika lead, tuba oompah bass, brass, zither and clarinet, with a simple cheerful major melody. Ländler lilt, clean acoustic recording, warm and homely. Perfect for a village festival.
