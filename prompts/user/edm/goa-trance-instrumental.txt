---
Genre: Goa Trance
Tempo: Fast (145-175 BPM)
Meter: 4/4 (common time)
Lyrics: instrumental
Length: 4-5 minutes
---

Classic 1990s Goa trance: layered acid 303 lines, Middle-Eastern-flavoured minor melodies, rolling 16th bass, analog arpeggios and cosmic pads. Long hypnotic build, a huge melodic breakdown around the middle, then an ecstatic layered climax. Psychedelic, spiritual and endlessly evolving.
