Smooth progressive trance: hypnotic rolling bass, crisp minimal drums, layered plucks and pads, a gradually unfolding melodic theme and long filter automations. Restrained rather than euphoric, with a deep atmospheric breakdown and a warm, driving second half. Wide, clean and hypnotic.
