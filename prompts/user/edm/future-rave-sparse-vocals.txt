Genre: Future Rave
Tempo: Uptempo (130-145 BPM)

Lyrics: sparse
Language: English

Dark, powerful festival future rave: huge distorted bass, relentless driving kick, tense hypnotic synth riff, dramatic build-ups with risers and snare rolls, explosive drop. Only processed vocal phrases as rhythmic hooks and effects, never full verses. Cinematic, energetic and instantly recognizable, mixed loud and wide.
