Late-1990s nu metal: seven-string downtuned guitar riffs, heavy syncopated groove, slapped and sliding bass, turntable scratches, subtle samples and drums with a hip-hop swing. Rapped and half-shouted verses, big melodic sung chorus, quiet whispered bridge. Lyrics about alienation, anger and not being heard.
