---
Genre: Doom Metal
Tempo: Slow (40-70 BPM)
Meter: 4/4 (common time)
Lyrics: instrumental
Length: 4-5 minutes
---

Slow, massive doom metal: crushing low-tuned downtuned guitars, thick fuzz bass, huge slow drums and a haunting melodic lead guitar. Dark recurring riff motifs, long sustained chords, enormous space between hits, gradual build to a monolithic climax. Mournful and cinematic rather than chaotic.
