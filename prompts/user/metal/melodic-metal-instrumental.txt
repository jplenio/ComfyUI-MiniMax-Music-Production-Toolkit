Genre: Melodic Metal
Tempo: Uptempo (130-145 BPM)

Powerful, highly melodic metal: heavy rhythm guitars, soaring harmonized lead guitar, tight bass and energetic double-kick drums. Strong recurring themes, dramatic build-ups, a lyrical clean-guitar middle section and a memorable extended solo. Heavy but beautiful, cinematic and uplifting rather than brutal.
