Warm, intimate folk ballad: fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft piano, cello and minimal brushed percussion, with a vulnerable close-mic vocal. Lyrics about finding an old letter from a past relationship and realizing the memories remain even though both lives have moved on. Simple poetic language, memorable restrained chorus, bittersweet rather than tragic.
