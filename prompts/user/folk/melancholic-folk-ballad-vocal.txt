Warm, intimate and organic, with fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft piano, cello and minimal brushed percussion. Vulnerable and deeply emotional. Lyrics about finding an old letter from a past relationship and realizing that the memories remain even though both lives have moved on. Simple poetic language, memorable restrained chorus, bittersweet rather than tragic.
