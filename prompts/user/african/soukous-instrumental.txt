---
Genre: Soukous
Tempo: Uptempo (130-145 BPM)
Meter: 4/4 (common time)
Lyrics: instrumental
Length: 4-5 minutes
---

Bright, fast Congolese Soukous: intertwining clean electric guitars playing rapid melodic sebene patterns, driving snare-and-hi-hat groove, busy congas, melodic bass and warm horn accents. Joyful, dancing and virtuosic, building from a relaxed rumba intro into an ecstatic uptempo section.
