Raw, dark South-African Gqom: broken minimal drum patterns, heavy detuned kick, hard tribal toms, thin ominous synth stabs and chanted percussive vocal fragments used as rhythm. Stripped-back, repetitive and physically heavy, with abrupt drops and long hypnotic percussion sections.
