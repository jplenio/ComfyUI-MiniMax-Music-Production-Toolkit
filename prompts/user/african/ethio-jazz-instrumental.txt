Hypnotic, modal and deeply soulful Ethio-Jazz: 1970s Ethiopian pentatonic scales over loose jazz harmony, breathy tenor saxophone lead, vibraphone, wah-guitar, Wurlitzer and swinging organic drums with a slight shuffle. Winding melodies, rich groove, mysterious timeless atmosphere and warm, slightly lo-fi analog recording.
