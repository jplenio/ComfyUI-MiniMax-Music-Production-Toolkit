Uplifting traditional gospel: powerful lead vocal answered by a full gospel choir, gospel piano with big chord runs, Hammond organ swells, walking bass, driving drums and tambourine. Call-and-response, hand claps, a modulation before the final chorus and an ecstatic vamp ending. Lyrics about gratitude, deliverance and joy.
